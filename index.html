Rumus Ringkas

KonsepRumus
KomplemenP(A') = 1 − P(A)
GabunganP(A ∪ B) = P(A) + P(B) − P(A ∩ B)
Saling lepasP(A ∩ B) = 0
IndependenP(A ∩ B) = P(A) × P(B)
BersyaratP(A|B) = P(A ∩ B) / P(B)
Probabilitas totalP(E) = P(E|H)·P(H) + P(E|H')·P(H')
Teorema BayesP(H|E) = P(E|H)·P(H) / P(E)
